How to play and scoring

How to play

  1. Tap Start level. After a 3-2-1 countdown your grid fills with the numbers 1 to 25 in a random order, and 3 tiles turn golden.
  2. A number is called at the top. Find it on your grid and tap it before the fuse empties.
  3. Clear every call in the level to unlock the next one. You can miss at most 2 calls; a 3rd miss fails the level and you retry it.
  4. Each level adds more calls and a shorter fuse: level 1 starts with 10 calls and a 10-second fuse, later levels run up to 25 calls with the fuse tightening to 4 seconds.
  5. Correct taps in a row build a multiplier, up to ×5. Every 7th call is a turbo call: a short fuse and double points. An 8-tap streak puts you on fire. Every 6 calls, the unmarked tiles shake into new positions.

Scoring

  1. Correct tap: (100 points plus 10 per second left) × your multiplier.
  2. Multiplier: grows by one every 4 correct taps in a row, up to ×5. A wrong tap or a missed call resets it.
  3. Turbo call, every 7th: a short fuse, and the tap pays double.
  4. Golden tile: +150 points and +1 star when you tap it as the called number.
  5. Line complete: +250 points and +1 star. 12 lines in all: 5 rows, 5 columns, 2 diagonals.
  6. Wrong tap: −25 points and the fuse loses 1 second. Missed call: the tile dims.
  7. Clean sweep, all 25 numbers tapped with no misses: +500 points.
  8. Your rank (D to S) follows your total score across cleared levels: S from 18000, A from 12000, B from 7000, C from 3000.
